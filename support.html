Support, LE Audio Setup & Beta Guide

Step-by-step instructions to confirm LE Audio stereo separation and submit Beta diagnostic logs.

1. How to Turn On LE Audio on Android

After pairing your earbuds, open Android Settings > Connected devices (or Bluetooth) > tap the gear icon next to your headset > enable 'LE Audio'. On Sony earbuds, also enable 'LE Audio Priority' in the Sony Sound Connect app. On Samsung devices, verify firmware updates and Bluetooth LE Audio toggles.

2. Understanding Stereo Verdicts

  • True Stereo Confirmed (L ≠ R): Only one meter spikes when tapping one bud (Separation > 55%). Ready for full 2-person Cross Intercom!
  • Duplicated Mono / Hardware 1 ch: Your phone or earbud firmware cloned one mic into both channels. Try switching AudioSource to UNPROCESSED and Usage to MEDIA in DSP Lab, then restart Intercom.
  • Speech cuts out or drops when screen locks: Set Android Battery Optimization for IntercomLE to 'Unrestricted', or lower/disable Noise Gate (slide toward -70 dBFS indoors).

3. Closed Beta Founding Member Instructions

If you received one of our 20 Reddit Beta Promo Codes: (1) Join through the official Play Console opt-in web link, (2) Install via Play Store, (3) Redeem your code in Play Store > Payments & subscriptions > Redeem code, (4) Keep the app installed continuously for 14+ days and open it briefly every 1–2 days.

4. Sending Feedback or Reporting a Bug

Open the Diagnostics tab inside IntercomLE and tap 'Copy Log'. Personal Bluetooth names and full MAC addresses are automatically masked. Paste the report along with your phone model and earbud model when emailing support or messaging us on Reddit.
