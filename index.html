Audio Engineering

Built like real DSP hardware, right on Android

Carefully tuned Kotlin audio loop aligned to Bluetooth LE Audio's 10 ms LC3 packet cadence.

10ms

10 ms LC3 Frame Alignment

Captures and writes 10 ms chunks with low-latency AudioTrack FIFO tightening and hardware underrun tracking.

200Hz

Moto Wind Filter (HPF)

Preset cuts engine & helmet wind rumble below 200 Hz while keeping human speech frequencies crisp and intelligible.

280ms

Anti-Chop Noise Gate

280 ms hold window, 5 dB hysteresis, and sample-rate-invariant smoothing keep syllable tails natural without dropouts.

L≠R

True Stereo Inspector

Instantly distinguishes true L ≠ R dual microphones from headsets whose firmware clones 1 mic into duplicated mono.

⚡

Anti-Clip Soft Limiter

Protects ears against sudden acoustic feedback spikes or loud wind blasts while supporting up to 250% voice gain.

🔒

Zero Cloud. Zero Servers.

Audio flows exclusively inside RAM between Bluetooth endpoints and Android AudioTrack. Nothing is ever recorded or uploaded.

Questions & Answers

Everything you should know before riding

Zero internet is required for intercom audio. All voice routing happens locally between your Bluetooth headset and phone memory. Internet is used solely by Google Play Billing when buying or restoring a license.
Legacy Bluetooth Classic (SCO/HFP) always merges mic capture into 1 mono stream. Even with Bluetooth LE Audio, true independent stereo capture depends on your phone chipset and earbud firmware exposing 2 input channels instead of cloning 1 mic to both sides.
Open Android Settings > Apps > IntercomLE > Battery and set it to 'Unrestricted' so your phone manufacturer doesn't throttle the microphone foreground service while locked in your pocket.
